## External data, tools, and report processing

Rules require external data, models, and tools to offer fair and reasonable access, and place limits around cost/accessibility; the host assesses unfair advantage. The rules also prohibit redistributing competition data to nonparticipants and incorporate the [RSNA MIRA license](http://rsna.org/mira-license). The full license was read on 2026-10-02 and contains a broad restriction on derivative works; its scope over competition-derived labels, features, or trained weights has not been clarified. Review provider terms and applicable access/cost constraints before selecting or operating an external service.

The [host discussion, “Use of Commercially Hosted LLMs”](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection/discussion/733965) was directly read. Its author had a Competition Host badge. The post dated 2026-08-09 says commercially hosted LLM services may process report text for label extraction subject to competition rules, reasonable access/cost, and provider terms. A host reply dated 2026-08-27 explicitly says LLM APIs may read reports and generate labels. This is conditional host permission, not blanket approval for every provider/workflow and not authorization to transmit this project's reports. D006 records that evidence. For the planned Gold-58 prompt comparison only, the user selected Codex CLI via ChatGPT subscription OAuth (no API key), targeting `gpt-6-luna` with `model_reasoning_effort=high`. Exact subscription entitlement remains unverified, and provider terms, data handling, and project authorization to transfer competition reports remain prerequisites; no report transfer is authorized by this plan.

## Licensing ambiguity and remaining unknowns

The rules page names CC-BY-NC 4.0 in the winner-license area and rule 2.5, while nearby text calls the license OSI-approved and says it must not restrict commercial use. Those statements conflict on their face. The MIRA license also has a broad no-derivative-work restriction whose application to labels, features, or weights remains unclear. Seek organizer clarification before distributing competition-derived artifacts or commercial deployment; do not resolve legal precedence here or select a project license. This ambiguity is not recorded as a blanket ban on private competition research, including research intended to inform a saleable product. It does not grant any additional permission to access or download competition data.

The rules do not resolve practical GPU/VRAM/RAM availability or establish a representative full-size inference runtime, and local full-schema/data-quality validation remains undone. Kaggle web acceptance/access is confirmed by the authenticated UI; API/CLI access is not established by that fact. Treat these as prerequisites for later inference/deployment planning, not as a claim of completed verification. Do not substitute generic platform defaults for these unknowns.
