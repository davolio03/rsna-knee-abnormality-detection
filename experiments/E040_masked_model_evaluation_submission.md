# E040 — Completed masked model versus constants and submission preparation

## Hypothesis and frozen baseline
The completed five-epoch fold-zero frozen ResNet18 should improve observed soft-report-label validation fit over a per-target constant fitted only on training observations. This is a cheap metadata/report check, not a new architecture or training experiment. The model uses the frozen E034 manifest/folds (2,850 train / 1,448 validation), Gold-58 exclusion, unweighted masked soft BCE, 256 px, eight slices, seed 42 and Adam 0.001. Competition comparison changes label source and cohort versus the old 500-study C2 model, so a score difference cannot isolate the effect of labeling.

## Protocol
Fit each constant as the mean observed soft target in training only, clip probabilities to [1e-12, 1-1e-12] for stable logarithms, evaluate held-out observed cells only and pool by cell count. Verify pinned manifest/fold membership and the exact final training-report digest/provenance/history before comparison. Preserve per-target exceptions; do not cast fractional labels into binary ground truth or manufacture AUC from aggregate losses. No image read, model loading or GPU evaluation is required.
