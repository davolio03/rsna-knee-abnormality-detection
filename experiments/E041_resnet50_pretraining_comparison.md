# E041 — Paired ResNet50 pretraining recipes

## Hypothesis and controlled comparison
Generic medical pretraining may improve masked soft-label knee learning relative to ImageNet at matched architecture and fine-tuning capacity. Both arms use ResNet50 with the same fixed4298-study cohort, fold0(2850/1448), Gold exclusion, seed42,224-pixel MRI resize, at most8 physically ordered slices per series and equal slice/series logit pooling. Only layer4 non-BatchNorm weights and the identical seeded2048-to12 head train; every BatchNorm affine parameter and statistic is frozen. Adam uses head1e-3/layer4 1e-5.

This compares pretraining recipes, not initialization alone: ImageNet mean/std versus RadImageNet2x-1 normalization. Both use common percentile-scaled MRI geometry, not ImageNet's canonical resize256/center-crop224 transform. Masked invalid pixels are zeroed after either normalization. Earlier ResNet18 baselines remain unchanged; their different architecture/resolution/trainable policy is not the isolated control.
